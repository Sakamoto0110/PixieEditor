implementation responsibility, status, dependencies and execution gates.
Its scope must fit the parent's approved outcome. Technical contracts stay
with their product owners; coordination ownership alone does not transfer them.

Pinned reference excerpts retain source coordinates and exact baseline digests,
and are explicitly nonauthoritative. They help read a shared parent's context;
they do not approve work, replace the parent or form a second mutable scheduler.
Existing work identities and recorded evidence retain their original provenance.

transferred Editor contributions. Completed local work leaves
